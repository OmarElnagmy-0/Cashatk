# Cashatk - Expense Tracker

Cashatk is a personal finance and expense tracking application designed to help users take full control of their daily spending. The app provides a clean, seamless, and intuitive experience for recording daily transactions, categorizing expenses, and understanding financial habits through clear visual summaries.

## 🌟 Key Features

* **User Onboarding:** A straightforward setup flow to personalize the user's profile.
* **Expense Management:** Quick and easy recording of daily expenses with amount, category, date, and custom notes.
* **Categorized Spending:** Structured organization of expenses across custom spending categories.
* **Financial Overview & Statistics:** Visual analytics and summaries to track total spending and monitor spending patterns over days, weeks, months, or years.
* **Transaction History & Details:** A comprehensive log of all recorded transactions with dedicated detail views for each entry.
* **Smart Filtering & Sorting:** Easy filtering and ordering of transactions by date and amount to track where money goes.
* **Local Security & Privacy:** Dedicated PIN code protection to keep personal financial records safe and private.
